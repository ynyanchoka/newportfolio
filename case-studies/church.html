Church admin platform

A church admin platform to streamline CRM, financial operations, and community management, improving communication, donation management, event organization, and content dissemination.

Problem

The church lacks an efficient administrative system, leading to difficulties in managing member communication, financial operations, and community events effectively.

Solution

Develop a comprehensive church admin platform to streamline CRM, financial operations, and community management, improving communication, donation management, event organization, and content dissemination for better church administration.

My role

I was the UI/UX designer for this project

Responsibilities

Conducted interviews, wireframing, prototyping, conducting usability studies, accounting for accessibility, iterating on designs, determining information architecture, and responsive design

My design process

USER RESEARCH: PAIN POINTS

Complex Event Management

Lack of centralized platform for event registration and management, resulting in confusion and difficulty in organizing community events.

Communication Challenges

Difficulty in communicating with members via email, SMS, and messaging apps, leading to poor engagement and coordination.

Tedious Financial Management

Manual processes for donation collection, reconciliation, and reporting, causing inefficiency and potential errors.
