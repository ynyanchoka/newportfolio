Trendy AI

Trendy AI connects influencers with campaign opportunities. However, early user feedback and analytics revealed issues with readability, inconsistent iconography, and suboptimal mobile experiences. The redesign aimed to improve usability and visual consistency while reducing cognitive load.

Problem

  • Inconsistent Visual Elements: Mixed icon styles in the side navigation created a disjointed experience.
  • Dashboard Clarity: Active and past campaigns were not clearly distinguished, and users had difficulty quickly assessing earnings and campaign status.
  • Mobile Responsiveness: The layout did not adapt well to smaller screens, limiting usability for influencers who are often on the go.
  • Campaign Interaction: The campaigns page lacked clear filtering and details, making it hard for influencers to determine which opportunities best fit their style and strengths.

Solution

Streamline the dashboard with clear sections for active/past campaigns and earnings, introduced a responsive two‑column layout, and enhanced the campaigns page with intuitive filtering and detailed campaign cards.
